# Luxury vehicle research — 5 November 2026

The local implementation adds these 20 model/body entries to the existing 20.
It preserves the original IDs and does not repeat their make/model combinations.
Each uses six colour variants in the existing Profile and map views, with the
generic car for a manual or unsupported selection. Backend and production
activation are verified separately.

## Selection and ranking

The latest model-level dataset located at research time is CarExpert's
[January–September 2026 Australian new-car sales](https://atlas.carexpert.com.au/latest-dataset).
It counts new registrations and is not a measure of the whole Australian fleet.
October headline totals have been published, but the detailed October model-level
data had not been located. Some rows combine different powertrains and others
split wagon and coupe bodies, so their figures must not be assigned to a single
specific illustrated variant.

The first 18 candidates are high-volume rows from established luxury marques
in that dataset; the final two are explicitly requested exotic marques. The
result is a curated list and **not** the 20 highest-selling luxury cars overall.
What qualifies as a luxury brand is subjective; brands including Zeekr, Denza
and Polestar are not included in this particular shortlist. MINI Cooper counts
cover multiple body styles. Years below denote the target Australian market
model year to research, not a guarantee of a specific trim or appearance.

Built-in image generation created one original 3×2, six-colour sprite sheet per
model using the existing icon as a style reference and a dated exterior image as
a geometry reference. The manifest records source sheets, reference URLs and the
prompt set; some sheets needed a background or badge cleanup. The reference
photographs are not included in the app. `scripts/prepare-vehicle-catalogue.mjs`
crops and compresses the approved sheets without repainting their pixels.
All 120 resulting 384×288 WebP assets were reviewed on a coloured background,
and the four 8×8 corner regions of each had alpha at most 10/255. The drawings
are stylised representations, not manufacturer-certified replicas; details may
vary between trims and the same registration year can include a model change.
