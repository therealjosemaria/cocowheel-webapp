The 2025 sales ranking chooses the model families; it does not establish the
year of an illustration. This table records the supported body-era coverage for
each drawing. These are simplified unbadged illustrations, not exact trim, wheel,
paint-code or VIN replicas. Minor equipment/wheel/grille-insert differences within
a body era are intentionally simplified; materially different bodies/facelifts
use Other and the generic car. No ten-year/five-year eligibility limit.

Years below are conservative enabled coverage, not a claim that a generation
ended in the final listed year. Do not extend coverage to a later facelift
without comparing its exterior. A selected year never comes from the email,
vehicle age, publication date or sales ranking.

## Cross-checks and corrections

All 20 drawings were researched individually and their final six-colour sheets
reviewed. The first pass assigned MY2025 too broadly; that assumption is superseded
by the explicit era mapping above. RAV4 uses the fifth-generation shape introduced
in 2019; Corolla uses the 2018 E210 hatch shape before the later grille revision.
The pictured Sportage uses boomerang DRLs, not the later facelift.

Manufacturer exterior photographs/brochures were directly compared for 18 models.
MG and Jolion manufacturer downloads did not provide usable exterior downloads;
authorised Australian dealer model-year photographs supplied the final visual
comparison, with manufacturer text/brochures used for model/generation context.
Those public reference photos remain temporary research files and are not shipped.

Geometry corrections replaced generic fronts for RAV4, Corolla, HiLux, D-Max,
Kona, Model Y, Shark 6, MU-X, MG ZS and Jolion. RAV4 and Corolla also received
camera-direction corrections. Latest source/prompt lineage is retained in the
[asset manifest](assets/vehicle-catalogue-sources.json).
